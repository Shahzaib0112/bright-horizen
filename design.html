FAQS

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before taking the first step into your tech career.

Still Have Questions?
Do I need prior experience to enroll?
+
No prior experience is required for our beginner tracks. We start from the very basics and gradually advance. For intermediate and advanced courses, a basic understanding of programming is recommended.
Are classes online or in-person?
+
We offer both! In-person classes are held at our Karachi campus, and live online sessions are streamed simultaneously for remote students. Recorded lectures are available for review anytime.
What certificate will I receive?
+
Upon successful completion of your course and final project, you receive an industry-recognized Bright Horizons certificate, along with a digital badge you can share on LinkedIn.
Is there job placement support?
+
Absolutely! We have an active career portal, regular job fairs, and dedicated placement counselors who help connect graduates with our network of 80+ hiring partner companies.
What are the payment options?
+
We offer flexible installment plans, scholarships for top performers, and early-bird discounts. Contact our admissions team for a personalized fee breakdown based on your chosen program.
How long are the courses?
+
Course durations range from 3 to 8 months depending on the program. Each course includes lectures, lab sessions, assignments, and a capstone project. Classes run 3–4 days per week.
